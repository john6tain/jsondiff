Privacy

Privacy policy

The short version: the JSON diff tool runs entirely in your browser. The JSON you compare never leaves your device, we have no accounts, we set no cookies, and we don't run analytics or tracking scripts.

What this tool does with your data

When you paste two payloads and press Compare JSON, both documents are parsed and compared locally, in your browser. The split view aligns lines with a local algorithm; the change list uses a diff library that is bundled into the page itself. Neither step sends your data anywhere.

You can verify this yourself: open your browser's developer tools, switch to the Network tab, compare two payloads, and watch. Comparing produces zero network requests. The only requests a page load ever makes are for static assets — the HTML, stylesheets, fonts, and scripts.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts or sign-ups. There is nothing to log into and no profile to create.
  • No cookies set by this site for the tool's operation.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts. We don't measure your usage with third-party trackers.
  • No server-side processing of your payloads. The web UI never posts JSON to a server for comparison. (A separate POST /api/diff endpoint exists for programmatic use; the site's UI does not call it, and using it is your choice.)
  • No retention of diffs. Nothing about a comparison is stored anywhere.

The URL parameters feature

The tool can pre-load payloads from the URL, e.g. /?left=%7B...%7D&right=%7B...%7D. Because values travel in the URL, they are transmitted to the server that hosts the page when your browser fetches it — that's inherent to how URLs work for any website. The server does not read, store, or log these values, and the comparison still happens locally. If you'd rather not have payloads appear in URLs, simply don't use the parameter feature — paste into the textareas instead.

Hosting and standard logs

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like essentially every host, the infrastructure sees routine connection data — your IP address, the page requested, and the time of the request — in order to serve the page. This is standard web-server operation, is not used to identify you, and does not include the contents of the JSON you compare.

Third-party content

Fonts. The site loads the Inter and JetBrains Mono typefaces from Google Fonts. When it does, your browser contacts Google's servers and transmits your IP address and user-agent string, as described in Google's privacy policy. No payload data is involved.

Ads. Advertising is currently disabled on this site. There are no live ad tags and no hidden ad placeholders on the pages. If ads are enabled in the future, Google AdSense (or another ad network) may place its own tags on content pages — never on or around the tool inputs — and those tags may use cookies and collect data in line with the ad network's own privacy policy. This section will be updated before any ads are turned on.

External links. Pages link to external sites (e.g. GitHub, Google). This policy applies only to this site; once you follow a link elsewhere, that site's policy governs.

Changes to this policy

If anything about the above changes — particularly around ads or analytics — this page will be updated and dated, and the change will be noted in the project's commit history.
